What the coverage is for

Your commercial auto and cargo policies protect the truck and the load. Life insurance protects the people who depend on you.

The truck note

Equipment loans don’t stop when the driver does. A death benefit can pay off what is owed, so your family isn’t forced to sell the truck in a hurry.

Your family’s income

Coverage sized to the years your family counts on your income: the mortgage, the kids, the bills a spouse would carry alone.

On or off the road

Occupational accident coverage usually pays only for accidents on the job. Life insurance pays whether it happens in the truck, at home or from an illness.

If you run drivers

When the business depends on you, coverage on your life can keep it running long enough to sell it or wind it down on your terms.

Questions

Is this the same as my truck insurance?

No. Commercial auto, cargo and occupational accident policies cover the truck, the load and injuries on the job. Life insurance pays your family a lump sum if you die, on the job or not.

Do I need a medical exam?

Some policies need one and some don’t. It depends on your age, the amount of coverage and your health. We tell you which applies before you apply.

Can it cover a loan on the truck?

Yes. Many owner-operators size coverage to at least the balance on their equipment loan plus the years of income their family needs. You name the beneficiary; it can be your spouse, a trust or, for a business loan, the business.

What does it cost?

It depends on your age, health, tobacco use, the amount of coverage and the type of policy. Tell us your age and a monthly amount that works for you, and we will show you what that buys from more than one carrier before you decide anything.

Can I change my mind after I buy?

Yes. Every state gives you a free-look period after the policy is delivered, usually 10 to 30 days depending on the state and the policy. Return it in that window and the premium you paid comes back. Your policy states the exact period on its cover page.
