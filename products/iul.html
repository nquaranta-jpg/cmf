Why Choose Indexed Universal Life

IUL combines permanent life insurance protection with a powerful cash value accumulation strategy.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is IUL? +

Indexed Universal Life is permanent life insurance with a cash value component that earns interest based on a stock market index, with a guaranteed minimum (floor). It combines life insurance protection with a savings and growth component.

Can I lose money? +

No. The 0% floor means your cash value never goes negative due to market performance. In years when the index is down, you simply earn 0% instead of taking a loss.

What are living benefits? +

If you're diagnosed with a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness, you can access a portion of your death benefit while you're still alive to help cover medical expenses or other needs.

How is the cash value taxed? +

Cash value grows tax-deferred. Policy loans are generally tax-free, making IUL one of the most tax-efficient ways to accumulate and access wealth.

Who is IUL best for? +

People who want permanent coverage plus a savings and investment component with downside protection. It's ideal for those looking to build tax-advantaged wealth while maintaining life insurance protection.
