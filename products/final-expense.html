FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a medical exam? +

No. Our final expense plans are simplified issue or guaranteed issue. You answer a few health questions on the application, but no physical exam, blood work, or doctor visits are required.

What ages qualify? +

Final expense insurance is available for individuals aged 50 to 85 years old.

How much coverage can I get? +

Coverage amounts range from $5,000 to $25,000, depending on your age and health answers.

Will my rate ever increase? +

Never. Your premium is locked in for life from the day your policy is issued. It will never go up regardless of age or health changes.

How fast is approval? +

Most applicants are approved within 24–48 hours. Some guaranteed issue policies provide same-day approval.
