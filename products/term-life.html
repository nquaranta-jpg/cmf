FAQ

Common Questions

What is term life insurance? +

Coverage for a set period (term) at a fixed premium. If you pass away during the term, your beneficiaries receive the death benefit. It's the most affordable type of life insurance.

What ages qualify? +

Term life insurance is available for individuals aged 18 to 75 years old.

How fast is the approval? +

Instant decision on most applications. You can find out if you're approved the same day you apply.

Can I convert to permanent insurance later? +

Yes, many term policies include a conversion option that allows you to convert to a permanent life insurance policy without a new medical exam.

How much coverage do I need? +

A common rule of thumb is 10–12x your annual income. We'll help you determine the right amount based on your family's specific needs during your consultation.
