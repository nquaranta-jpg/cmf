Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Crown Merchant Financial LLC (“we,” “us,” or “Crown Merchant Financial”) operates crownmerchantfinancial.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you request a quote, download a guide, or book a call, we collect details you provide such as your name, phone number, email address, age range, desired coverage amount, and product interest.
  • Information collected automatically: like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies (including advertising pixels from Meta, Google, Microsoft, TikTok, and OpenAI) that collect device information, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site (for example, which ad you clicked).

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request or guide download and provide the services you asked for.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request. By submitting a form with your phone number, you consent to receive calls and texts from Crown Merchant Financial at that number, including via automated technology. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or tell us on any call.
  • To obtain quotes on your behalf from licensed insurance carriers.
  • To measure and improve our advertising (for example, telling an ad platform that a form was submitted).

How we share information

  • Insurance carriers: to obtain the quotes and coverage you request.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the site and our business (such as website hosting, data storage, scheduling, and the advertising platforms listed above).
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and advertising

Our advertising pixels help us understand which ads lead to quote requests. Some (such as Meta’s) receive limited contact information in hashed form to match conversions. You can limit ad tracking through your browser settings and the ad-preference tools offered by each platform.

Data retention and security

We keep lead and client information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet insurance record-keeping requirements. Information is stored with access controls, and we limit who can view it.

Your choices and rights

  • You may opt out of calls or texts at any time (reply STOP or just tell us).
  • You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing nquaranta@crownmerchantfinancial.com.
  • Depending on your state, you may have additional privacy rights; we honor verified requests consistent with applicable law.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
