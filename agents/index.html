Why Agents Choose CMF

Questions

Common questions.

Is there a non-compete?

No. Our agreement relies on non-solicitation and confidentiality instead, and we have a fast-release policy if you decide to leave.

How are agents paid?

CMF agents are independent contractors (1099) paid by commission.

What do I need to onboard?

Your insurance license information, NPN, driver’s license, a voided check or bank letter for direct deposit, and your E&O certificate. The packet takes about 15 to 20 minutes online.

What is CMF Match?

Our program for producers who want their own agency. Commit two years, exceed the production threshold, and CMF invests $50,000 in equity to launch your agency inside our downline. Full terms are being finalized. Learn more
