## Build Notes (not page copy)

1. The consent language is drafted, not approved. TCPA and state telemarketing rules are unforgiving and the fines are per-message. Have the exact checkbox text reviewed before the first ad dollar. Do not copy Aria's version that claims consent applies "even if on a DNC list": consented contact with proper disclosure can permissibly reach DNC-registered numbers, but that phrasing invites scrutiny.
2. IUL compliance is stricter than anything on the FE side, and it applies to this page and the training itself. Never use "investment," "tax-free retirement plan," "guaranteed growth," or market-return comparisons in the page copy, the ads pointing here, or the recorded training. Illustrations are governed by AG 49-B and carriers actively police how their IUL products are characterized. Keep carrier names off this page entirely: several carriers require advertising review of any material referencing their products, and an unnamed "carrier shelf" reference needs no approval. The training script itself should get a compliance read before recording, because a recorded video is discoverable marketing material.
3. No income claims anywhere on this page. The promise is a submitted application in 7 days, which is achievable and stated as a target. Note the deliberate wording: submitted, not issued. IUL is underwritten and issue timelines run weeks, so promising a 7-day issued policy would be false. If the main landing page's 7-day first policy target is kept, it should be understood as an FE-speed target, and this page's wording should not be changed to match it.
4. The training itself has to exist and be good. Record the real 45 minutes: the actual day-one IUL onboarding, cleaned up. The six modules above are the outline. A weak video after a strong opt-in page burns the list.
5. Funnel wiring: Meta ad → this page → instant email + SMS with the training link → 3 to 5 touch follow-up sequence in CMF Desk → push to free account → onboarding call booking. The follow-up sequence is where the opt-in becomes a recruit; write it next.
6. Stat blocks stay empty until real. The credential story (Navy, Guggenheim, six carriers, working producer) carries the trainer section until defensible numbers exist, and it is more differentiated than another self-reported production figure anyway.
7. Positioning coherence check: the main CMF Launch landing page currently says "purpose built for final expense and term telesales." With IUL as the flagship training, decide whether the platform's stated vertical widens (final expense, term, and IUL) or whether IUL becomes the headline. One sentence to change either way, but the two pages should agree before launch.
