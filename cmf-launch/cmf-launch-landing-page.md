## FAQ

Questions agents actually ask.

Is the free account actually free?
Yes. It takes 60 seconds, needs no credit card, and shows you the whole platform, including live lead inventory in your state. You pay only when you order leads.

Do I need experience?
No. The training assumes a license and a work ethic, nothing else. If you can follow a playbook and make calls, the system is built for you.

What can I write?
Final expense, term, whole life, and IUL, by phone. Crown Merchant Financial is an independent agency appointed with six carriers.

Are the leads really exclusive and at cost?
Yes. Leads are generated for CMF agents, sold once, and priced at cost. The live inventory in your account shows exactly what is available in your state before you spend a dollar.

Do I have to commit to anything to look?
No. The free account comes with no commitment. Look around, watch the training, check the lead inventory, and decide with everything in front of you.
