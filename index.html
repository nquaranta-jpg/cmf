About Us

About Crown Merchant Financial

Crown Merchant Financial LLC is an independent insurance agency founded by two U.S. military veterans and University of Chicago alumni. We are licensed nationwide and headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.

We work as a broker, not a carrier. We compare products from multiple A-rated insurers on your behalf and recommend the policy that fits your goals, health, and budget, whether that is final expense coverage, term life, indexed universal life, or a fixed annuity. Through our capital desk, we also arrange business-purpose lending for real estate investors.
