What's Covered

Final Expense Insurance Coverage

A whole life insurance policy designed to cover end-of-life expenses so your family never has to worry about the financial burden.

Funeral & Burial Costs

Cover the full cost of funeral services, casket, burial plot, headstone, and all related arrangements.

Outstanding Medical Bills

Ensure medical bills from your final illness don't become your family's burden. Coverage pays directly to your beneficiaries.

Remaining Debts

Credit cards, personal loans, and other outstanding debts won't transfer to your loved ones. Your policy handles them.

Family Financial Protection

Provide a financial cushion for your spouse or children during a difficult time. Benefits are paid tax-free to your beneficiaries.

Guaranteed Acceptance

Locked-In Premiums
