Our Thesis

Ask the Better Question

Most people shop for a price. We start with the risk. Four questions we ask every client before we quote anything.

92M U.S. adults who say they need life insurance or more coverage than they have.1

What would your family run out of first?

A policy is a number on paper until you test it against the mortgage, the payroll, and the years your kids still need you. We size coverage to the gap, not to a round number, and compare term, whole life, and indexed options across carriers.

Size My Gap → How term life works
4M+ Americans turning 65 each year in the “Peak 65” window, 2024 through 2027.2

What if your savings had to last 30 years?

Retirement is no longer a finish line. It is a funding problem that can run three decades. Fixed annuities and indexed universal life can add guaranteed or protected value alongside Social Security, and we show you exactly what is guaranteed and what is not.

Explore Annuities → How IUL builds cash value
5 yrs How often VGLI premiums step up as you age into a new bracket.3

What happens to your coverage after the uniform comes off?

SGLI ends when service does. VGLI accepts you without a health exam, but the price climbs every five years for life. Healthy veterans can often lock a level rate with a private carrier instead. We are veterans too, and we will run both side by side.

Run the VGLI Calculator → Join CMF Society
1 Owner. For most small businesses, that one person is the whole plan.

Who pays the note if the operator can’t?

Owner-operators and small business owners carry the equipment loan, the house, and the family on one income. Life insurance with living benefits can cover the debt, replace the income, and keep the business from being sold off in a hurry.

Our Perspective

We look at your household the way an investment firm looks at a balance sheet.

Before Crown Merchant Financial, our founder worked in sales and trading at Guggenheim. The lesson that carried over is simple: understand the risk before you price it. Every household has assets, liabilities, and a funding gap.

Most agents start with a product. We start with the gap, then find the carrier and policy that close it at the best price your health allows.

  • Independent by designWe place business with multiple A-rated carriers. No captive quota, no house product to push.
  • Facts before the quoteWe gather your health, budget, and existing coverage first, so the number you see is built on your real situation.
  • Built for the long runAnnual reviews, beneficiary updates, and help at claim time. The relationship starts when the policy is issued.

About Crown Merchant Financial

Crown Merchant Financial LLC is an independent, veteran-owned insurance agency founded by Nicholas Quaranta, a U.S. Navy veteran and University of Chicago graduate. We are headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and our founder is licensed in all 50 states.
