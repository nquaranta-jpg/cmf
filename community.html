Available Workshops

Two sessions built for older adults and veterans.

Each runs about 30 minutes with time for questions. We can present in person or virtually, and we work around your calendar.

For Older Adults

Spotting Today's Scams

30 minutes · In person or virtual

Scams aimed at seniors have changed fast. This session walks through the ones hitting hardest right now, including the new AI "cloned voice" calls that sound exactly like a grandchild in trouble, and the handful of simple habits that stop almost all of them.

  • How AI voice-cloning and "grandparent" calls actually work
  • The red flags in gift card, wire, and "act now" demands
  • Calling back on a known number and setting a family safe word
  • What to do, and who to call, if something already happened

A good fit for: senior centers, YMCAs and active-aging programs, libraries, and faith communities.

For Veterans & Families

Understanding Your Veteran Benefits

30 minutes · In person or virtual

Many veterans and surviving spouses leave earned benefits on the table simply because no one explained them plainly. This session lays out the major categories in everyday language and points out the common gaps families discover too late.

  • A plain-language map of the major benefit categories
  • Survivor and dependent benefits that often go unclaimed
  • Where coverage gaps tend to hide for veteran families
  • Where to turn for official help and how to get started

A good fit for: American Legion and VFW posts, veteran service organizations, and military family groups.
