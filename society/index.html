Questions

Common Questions

Does membership cost anything?

No. Society membership and workshops are open to all at no charge. Society Plus will be an optional paid tier offered at the same price to everyone.

Do I need a policy to join?

No. Everyone is welcome.

Will I be sold anything at workshops?

Workshops are educational. No purchase is ever required. If you would like a private appointment afterward, we are glad to book one.

What are Client Services?

Client Services are how we service the policies we write, including annual reviews and beneficiary audits. They come with being a Crown Merchant Financial client.

Who runs the Society?

Crown Merchant Financial LLC, an independent life insurance agency.
