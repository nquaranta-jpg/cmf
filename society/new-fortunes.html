New Fortunes Are Built in Insurance

Why the world’s biggest investors are buying insurance companies, and where independent agents fit.

Tuesday, December 1, 2026 · 6:30 to 8:00 PM · 5113 S. Harper Ave., Hyde Park, Chicago

The Evening

Follow the money, then see the work.

Over the last decade, firms like Apollo and KKR built some of the largest businesses in finance by owning insurance companies. This evening looks at why, what it says about where the industry is heading, and what it means for the people who actually sell the policies.

Then we open the hood: how most agencies structure their agents, the traps new agents walk into, and a live look at the tools CMF agents use every day.
