FAQ

Common Questions

Are the leads exclusive? +

Yes. Leads in your package are delivered to you and only you. We don't resell or recycle them to other agents on the platform.

What do the tiers cost? +

Starter, Pro, and Elite packages are priced by lead volume and type. See the lead packages page for current tiers, or send an inquiry and we'll walk you through what fits your budget.

Do I have to be a CMF agent to buy? +

No. CMF Launch is open to licensed insurance agents industry-wide. Leads, ads management, and training don't require joining CMF. CMF Match is the exception — it's built around a two-year commitment to CMF.

How does billing work? +

Lead packages are purchased up front. Managed ads clients pay a monthly management fee plus their own ad spend. Training is billed per engagement. There is no free tier.

What is the CMF Match production threshold? +

Full terms — including the threshold and the structure of the $50k equity investment — are being finalized and will be published soon. Apply now and you'll be first to see them.

Can I cancel? +

Managed ads and training are month-to-month — cancel anytime before your next billing cycle. Purchased lead packages are delivered in full and aren't refundable once fulfillment starts.
